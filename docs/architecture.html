What has to be true before the app tells a golfer anything

Most of this system is refusals. A shot arrives from a Rapsodo CSV and passes seven gates before it can become a drill — and at every one, the honest output is often silence. This is what each gate checks and why it exists.

The decision pipeline

Read top to bottom. Anything stopped at a gate produces an explanation, never a number with a confidence it hasn't earned.

1 · Parse
CSV → shot objects
CSVParser maps Rapsodo's 18 columns. A blank or unparseable numeric cell becomes null, never 0 — Rapsodo leaves club data empty when it isn't picked up, and coercing that to zero is what once scored a shot with no measurements as a perfect strike.
drops nothing — but marks what was never measured
2 · Stamp
Attach the measurement context
Store.stamp() writes ball type, surface and alignment state onto every shot, at both entry points — loading and fresh import. Downstream gates work on flattened shot lists, so no call site has to thread session context, and none can forget to.
holds ball ≠ RPT · surface unknown · alignment unconfirmed
3 · Tier
Is this metric allowed to prescribe?
Metrics.TIER. Tier 1 prescribes freely (club speed, ball speed, smash, carry). Tier 2 displays only (launch angle, attack angle, club path). Tier 3 never prescribes — spin, spin axis, launch direction, and every modelled output.
blocks tier 3 from every prescription path, on any ball
4 · Sample
Are there enough shots to say anything?
10 shots of a club before any mean is reported. 15 before a club-path or attack-angle claim. 30 for dispersion tails — dropping to 10 when alignment is confirmed, because that gate removes bias rather than noise.
blocks below Metrics.MIN_SHOTS_REPORT, or a rule's own minShots
5 · Recur
Is this a pattern or is it the radar?
FaultEngine reports a fault only when it recurs at a rate noise would not produce: 2+ affected shots, 30%+ of that club's shots, judged against the clubs it appeared on rather than the whole session. Below 50% it reports as tentative with severity downgraded.
blocks 1-of-12 · flags 5-of-12 · passes 9-of-12
6 · Weight
How much practice time does it earn?
PracticePlan scales by severity × scoring weight × session share × confidence. Approach clubs outrank a 3-wood hit twice a round, because that is where strokes-gained puts scoring differences. Every block prescribes balls as well as minutes.
Output: a drill, with an external-focus cue, a ball count and a ~70% success target.
7 · Retain
Did it actually work?
RetentionProbe opens on the prescription and settles against a session 20 hours to 10 days later. This is the efficacy metric — not within-session change, which the evidence says is the one window that cannot see learning.
verdict retained · regressed · no detectable change · unknown
The gate that cannot be averaged away

Six of these gates handle noise, which shrinks as √n. Alignment handles bias, which does not shrink at all. A unit aimed 2° right makes a straight swing read 2.4° open once face-to-path amplifies it by 1/R — identically at 10 shots and at 30, while every other gate grows more confident. That asymmetry is why setup is a checklist rather than a tolerance.

Invariants

Properties that hold everywhere. Each has a test that fails if it stops holding.

intervalsEvery ± is computed from the golfer's own shots. No population constant reaches a displayed number; Metrics.mdc() is reachable only from the Settings reference document.
device errorTreated as zero — not because it is, but because an observed spread already contains it, and adding a constant on top counts the same error twice.
spinNever drives a drill, on any ball. With an RPT ball it describes a session; without one every spin figure is suppressed and replaced with why.
face angleDerived, never presented as a measurement. A suspected off-centre strike invalidates it for that shot rather than quietly widening it.
cuesExternal focus only — club, ball, turf, target. A test fails if a cue naming the golfer's own body parts is added.
verdictsThree-valued. A change smaller than the golfer's variation is "no detectable change", never "no improvement" — the app cannot tell those apart and says so.

Working on it

npm test runs a load gate — app.js executed whole in jsdom against the real index.html, then a reachability probe for late declarations — before any suite. A top-level throw leaves every later const in the temporal dead zone, so the app is dead while node --check still passes. That is not hypothetical: it shipped, and survived four commits behind a harness that regex-extracted modules and never ran the file.

If the gate fails, the suites do not run. They would be meaningless.

Before changing a sourced constant

The face ratios, spin-loft weights, tier assignments and sample floors are not guesses. Each is cited in docs/research-base-v2.md or docs/coaching-calibration-audit.html, and CLAUDE.md lists which section covers which. The reasoning is why they hold — read it before moving a number.
